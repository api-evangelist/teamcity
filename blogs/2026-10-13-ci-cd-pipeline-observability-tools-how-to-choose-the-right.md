When a CI/CD pipeline fails, its dashboard can usually show you what went wrong. Pipeline observability tools should help you understand why, whether the cause is a flaky test, an overloaded build agent, or a problem elsewhere in the delivery process. This becomes harder as pipelines grow more complex.
