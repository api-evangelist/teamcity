CI/CD remains one of the foundations of modern software development. According to the JetBrains Developer Ecosystem Survey 2026, 83.4% of respondents who use DevOps practices run CI/CD pipelines. The new report looks at how teams are running those pipelines, which tools they use, and where AI fits into their workflows.
