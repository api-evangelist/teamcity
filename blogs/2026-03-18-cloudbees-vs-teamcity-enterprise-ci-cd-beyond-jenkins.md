Many organizations adopt Jenkins because it’s flexible and widely supported. Over time, however, that flexibility often turns into operational overhead: maintaining plugins, debugging pipelines, and coordinating upgrades across teams. CloudBees CI and JetBrains TeamCity represent two different ways of addressing this problem.
