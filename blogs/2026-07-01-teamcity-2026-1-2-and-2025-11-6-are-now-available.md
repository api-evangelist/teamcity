Two bug-fix releases for TeamCity On-Premises: 2026.1.2 resolves over 10 issues including build cancellation, Perforce checkouts, AWS credentials, and S3 bucket operations, while 2025.11.6 addresses security vulnerabilities only; both preserve data format compatibility within their series so no backup restoration is needed to upgrade or downgrade.
